## 1. Brand Voice Guidelines & Linguistic Rules

### The 4 Non-Negotiable Rules of Our Copy

1. **Speak Business, Not Syntax**:
   - ❌ *Don't say*: "Isomorphic SSR hydration optimization and bundle tree-shaking."
   - ✅ *Say*: "Your website loads in under 1 second on mobile, even on a regular Jio 4G connection, so ad clicks don't bounce."
2. **Lean Heavily on the Freelancer Advantage**:
   - ❌ *Don't say*: "Our multidisciplinary agency squad leverages full-stack synergies."
   - ✅ *Say*: "You talk directly to me. I am a senior engineer with years of production experience. No sales reps, no account managers, and no junior interns practicing on your project."
3. **Radical Ownership & Transparency**:
   - ❌ *Don't say*: "Proprietary managed cloud deployment retainer."
   - ✅ *Say*: "You own 100% of your code, domain, and hosting. Everything is registered in your name with zero lock-in."
4. **Transparent, Fair Pricing**:
   - Clear fixed pricing ranges in Indian Rupees (INR) with guaranteed turnaround times. No hidden change-request fees.
