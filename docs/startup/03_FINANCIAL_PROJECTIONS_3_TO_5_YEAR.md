## 6. Monthly Cash Flow & Working Capital Dynamics

### 6.1 The Negative Working Capital Engine
Website Remedies operates with an exceptional cash collection dynamic that completely eliminates working capital drag:

1. **Website Rescue**: 50% upfront commitment fee collected upon contract execution prior to any codebase inspection. 50% final balance collected within 48 hours of verified staging sign-off, prior to production cutover.
2. **Website Rebuild**: 40% upfront deposit on contract signature; 40% upon completion of staging review and Core Web Vitals audit pass; 20% on production DNS cutover.
3. **Website Scale Retainers**: Billed 100% in advance on the 1st of each calendar month via automated recurring credit card or NACH bank mandates. Zero work proceeds without verified clearance.
